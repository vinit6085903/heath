Inside the AI Pipeline

A transparent look into how Equinox Sphere's AI Health Predictor analyzes your symptoms through seven distinct stages to deliver clinical-grade insights.

1
keyboard

Enter Symptoms

User input collected via an intuitive NLP (Natural Language Processing) interface. We handle conversational descriptions and medical jargon with equal precision.

2
cleaning_services

Preprocessing

Data cleaning and noise reduction. Our filters remove irrelevant information and standardize medical terminology for the neural network.

3
segment

Tokenization

Breaking down complex symptoms into machine-readable tensors. Words are converted into multi-dimensional vectors that capture semantic meaning.

4
psychology

LSTM Prediction

Core neural network sequence processing. Long Short-Term Memory units analyze the temporal relationship between symptoms to identify patterns.

5
query_stats

Confidence Score

Probability calculation and uncertainty quantification. We don't just give an answer; we tell you exactly how certain the model is about its findings.

6
verified_user

Risk Assessment

Mapping predictions against clinical databases and global health guidelines to categorize the level of medical urgency.

7
description

Results Generation

Generation of a comprehensive diagnostic report, ready to be shared with your healthcare provider for professional verification.
