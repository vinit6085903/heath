Problem Statement

Addressing the global challenge of chronic disease prevalence through early predictive interventions. The clinical need for proactive health monitoring has never been greater.

Current diagnostic frameworks often react to symptoms rather than predicting them. Equinox Sphere bridges the gap between raw biometric data and actionable medical insights using time-series analysis.
